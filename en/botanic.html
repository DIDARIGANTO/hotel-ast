About

A flat instead of a room, for families and long stays

Every apartment has a kitchen with cookware, a hob and a fridge, so breakfast and dinner can be cooked at home instead of hunting for a cafe. A washing machine and a place to work take away the hotel feeling: in a flat like this people happily stay a week or a month.

The complex stands on Turkestan St., in a quiet part of Esil, next to the Botanical Garden.
