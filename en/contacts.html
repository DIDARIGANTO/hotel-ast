Staying with us

Terms of stay

Below are guidelines for all three hotels in the group: the exact terms are confirmed by the hotel when you book. At Botanic Garden Apartments the stay is charged by the actual arrival and departure time.

  • Check-in usually from 14:00; early check-in agreed in advance
  • Check-out usually by 12:00; late check-out agreed in advance
  • An ID card or passport for every guest
  • The payment method and a company invoice are agreed when you book
  • Places for children and an extra bed are agreed when you book — state the ages of the children in your request
  • Staying with pets is arranged in advance
  • Quiet in the rooms and corridors at night

The final terms of the stay are given by the hotel when it confirms the booking; the full text of the rules is shown at the front desk.
