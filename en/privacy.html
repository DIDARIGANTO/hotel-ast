Personal data processing policy

How the AST Hotels group receives and uses the data of guests who submit requests on the website.

Who processes the data

The operator is the AST Hotels group, which unites AMINA Hotel and City Line Hotel (15 Shamshi Kaldayakov St., Astana) and Botanic Garden Apartments (14a Turkestan St., Astana). A request from the website goes to the hotel you selected and is handled by its front desk.

What data we receive

Only what you enter in the forms yourself: name, phone, city, check-in and check-out dates, number of guests, the selected category and a comment. The website does not ask for ID documents or bank card details.

What they are used for

To contact you about a booking request and confirm dates and price; to reply to a message sent through the contact form. The data is not used for marketing mailings.

Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent, which you give by ticking the box before submitting the form (Articles 7 and 8 of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan “On Personal Data and Its Protection”). Without consent the form is not sent — you can always call or message the hotel on WhatsApp directly instead.

Where the data goes

The request is sent as a WhatsApp message to the selected hotel’s number. The message passes through the WhatsApp service of Meta Platforms, which processes it under its own privacy policy. The website itself stores nothing: it has no server side and no request database.

How long it is kept

The hotel keeps the correspondence for as long as needed to fulfil the booking, but no longer than three years after the last contact. At your request the data is deleted earlier.

Your rights

You can find out what data the hotel holds about you, correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time — by messaging or calling the hotel you sent the request to. The request is fulfilled within three working days.

Cookies and browser storage

The website uses no cookies and no analytics trackers. The browser stores only the selected interface language — a technical value that is never transmitted anywhere.
