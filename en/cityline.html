Location

15 Shamshi Kaldayakov St., Astana

The hotel is in a modern district on the right bank, in the same building as AMINA Hotel. The National Museum and the LRT stations are within walking distance, and the business centre on the left bank is a few minutes away by car across the bridge. Transfers are arranged on request.
