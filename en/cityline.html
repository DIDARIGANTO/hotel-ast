About

Fewer rooms, more attention for every guest

City Line is a boutique hotel at 15 Shamshi Kaldayakov St.: with only a few rooms, the front desk has time for every guest. The focus is comfort, style and home-like warmth — rooms furnished like living spaces, not standard hotel units.

The city hotel AMINA works in the same building: if we have no free rooms, we will point you to the dates and categories our neighbours have — their terms are their own, and their manager will give them in the chat.

Location

Sarayshyk, between the embankment and the business district

The hotel is in Sarayshyk, the quarter between the Esil embankment and Mangilik El Avenue. In the morning it is easy to get to meetings in the left-bank business centres; in the evening you can walk along the water. Transfers from the airport and the railway station are arranged on request, and 2GIS is the easy way to plan a route across the city.
