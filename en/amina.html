About

Exactly what a business trip or a short weekend calls for

AMINA means quiet rooms with heavy curtains and solid sound insulation, a front desk that never closes and a breakfast that adapts to your departure time. The hotel is at 15 Shamshi Kaldayakov St., in the same building as the boutique hotel City Line. If we have nothing free on your dates, we will tell you what our neighbours have.

Guests who stay longer are offered special long-stay terms — we will explain them in chat or over the phone.

Location

Saraishyq, the business quarter of the left bank

The hotel is in Sarayshyk, the quarter between the Esil embankment and Mangilik El Avenue. From here it is easy to reach both the left-bank business centres and the city sights: 2GIS builds the route from the hotel door. Transfers from the airport and the railway station are arranged on request.
