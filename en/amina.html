What is included

We have taken care of the essentials so that nothing distracts you during your trip:

  • Free Wi-Fi

    High-speed internet throughout the hotel for work and staying in touch.

  • Guest parking

    A convenient parking area right by the hotel for those travelling by car.

  • 24-hour front desk

    Our staff are on site around the clock and ready to check you in at any hour.

  • Shared kitchen

    A fully equipped area with a hob, fridge, microwave, kettle and a full set of crockery, where you can store food and cook or reheat a meal at any time.
