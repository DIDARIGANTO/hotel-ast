How to use the offers

Send a request from the site or write on the WhatsApp of the hotel you want to stay at. The manager confirms the free dates and fixes the terms of the offer in the chat — no separate promo code is needed.

Offers do not combine: one applies to a booking — the one that is better for the guest. Discounts do not apply to extras: breakfast, transfer, parking, late check-out.

The cancellation terms are given by the hotel when it confirms the booking — they depend on the dates and the category. To cancel, write on the WhatsApp of the hotel you booked with and the manager will confirm the cancellation in a reply.
